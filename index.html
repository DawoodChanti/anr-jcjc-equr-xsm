01 — THE PROJECT

How reliable is a prediction?
And its explanation?

Medical-image models can be confident when they are wrong. EQUR-XSM aims to quantify this uncertainty and make visual explanations more informative for clinicians.

The project connects Evidential Deep Learning (EDL), confidence calibration and explainable AI (XAI). Its research programme addresses three complementary questions.

01.1

Calibrated confidence

Develop uncertainty estimates that better reflect prediction reliability, with the aim of limiting errors associated with overconfidence.

01.2

Uncertainty in explanations

Enrich saliency maps with pixel-level uncertainty to indicate both influential image regions and the reliability of their interpretation.

01.3

Multimodal segmentation

Model uncertainty specific to each imaging modality, such as MRI or CT, when combining information for segmentation.

Target applications include tumours, skeletal muscle abnormalities and haematological diseases. The project description envisages clinical validation with the university hospitals of Grenoble and Nantes, and the use of open data to support reproducibility.

Research objectives, not demonstrated clinical outcomes. Sources: ANR project record · GIPSA-lab project page.

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dawood Al Chanti

Associate Professor · Grenoble INP – Phelma, UGA
Permanent researcher · GIPSA-lab, Data Science Department

His research combines computer vision and machine learning for medical-image analysis, with interests in segmentation, uncertainty quantification, multimodal learning and explainable AI.

He earned a PhD in Image and Signal Processing at Université Grenoble Alpes in 2019, then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at LS2N, École Centrale de Nantes, before joining Grenoble INP in 2021.
